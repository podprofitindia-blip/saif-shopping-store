Kitchen Storage & Pantry Organization Guide

A tidy kitchen starts with choosing storage that suits what you keep and the space you have. Use these simple tips when comparing jars, containers and baskets.

1. Choose the right container

Use airtight containers for dry pantry staples when suitable, and check the product listing for material, capacity, lid type and care instructions before buying.

2. Group similar items together

Keep spices, snacks, baking ingredients and everyday essentials in separate groups. Baskets can help gather small packets, while jars make frequently used dry goods easier to see.

3. Measure your shelves first

Measure shelf height, width and depth before choosing jars or containers. Check product dimensions on the retailer's page so items fit your cupboard or pantry.

4. Compare current details

Compare capacity, material, cleaning instructions and the number of pieces in a set—not only the displayed discount. Prices, ratings, discounts and availability can change, so confirm the latest details on Myntra before ordering.
